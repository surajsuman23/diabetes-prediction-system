Methodology, data & limitations

Leakage-aware evaluation

Median imputation and scaling are fitted inside each training fold. Selected zero measurements are treated as missing; zero pregnancies remains valid. Hyperparameters are selected by training recall.

Scope of the data

This historical dataset represents a restricted population. One split does not establish real-world or clinical performance. This app evaluates a dataset; it does not provide patient predictions.
